Why This Project is Required

Stroke is one of the leading causes of death and permanent disability worldwide. Early detection is extremely important because delayed diagnosis can lead to severe brain damage or death.

Millions of stroke cases go undetected or are diagnosed too late due to shortage of radiologists, manual analysis delays, and lack of advanced healthcare facilities in many areas.
